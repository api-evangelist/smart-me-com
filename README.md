smart-me AG provides cloud‑based energy management solutions for buildings and enterprises. Their platform integrates smart meters, electric vehicle charging stations, solar generation and multi‑energy assets into a unified dashboard, offering real‑time data, automated billing, and tools for optimizing consumption and reducing costs. Based in Rotkreuz, Switzerland, smart‑me serves the Swiss market with a focus on data security, storing information locally in Switzerland.

**Areas:** Company · Energy · IoT · Cloud · Smart-Meter
